# `outreach-generator`

- **Provider:** `Codex`
- **Run location:** `on this computer`
- **Room:** `#citebridge-room`


## Description
Drafts personalized partnership proposals for top-scored partners and posts them in the room for founder approval.


## Agent Instructions
```text
  1. Take the highest-scoring partners from the Partner Evaluator Agent.
  2. Draft a concise, personalized collaboration proposal for each top partner that highlights mutual value (e.g., co-marketing, content swap, product integration, or community workshop).
  3. Post the ready-to-send proposal into the room asking:
     "Founder, here is a partnership proposal for [Partner Name]. Would you like me to approve this or make adjustments?"
```
